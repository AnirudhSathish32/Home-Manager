 Missing fields are null; never assume a quantity of one. Some receipts print one item across two lines, such as a department or name line with the price and a line with a product code and name: combine them into one row citing both lines, use the product name as description, the number beside it as product_code, and the price as line_total. A count printed on its own line under an item (9 @ $4.19 ea, 2 @ 1.50) belongs to the item above it: quantity is the count, unit_price the price each, line_total the item's printed amount, citing both lines; it is never an item of its own. Department headings alone (GROCERY, HEALTH AND BEAUTY) are not items. tax_code: many receipts print a tax code beside each price (such as BF, T, A, N); copy it exactly as printed, or null when none is printed. taxed: true or false only when the receipt says what the item's code means (a legend such as T = TAXABLE, or a tax line printed for its code); a food-stamp or EBT mark such as F or E never means untaxed on its own; otherwise null. returned: true for an item brought back for a refund on a return or exchange receipt (marked RETURN, REFUND or printed negative), false for an item bought. Copy its line_total as printed. A tip, gratuity or service charge is never an item.
