# Donating documents to test Home Manager

Thank you for considering this. Please read it all before you send anything. If something here isn't clear, ask me first.

## What I'm asking for

Copies of a few of your receipts, bank or card statements, or pay stubs. For each one, you check what Home Manager read from it and mark each value as correct or fix it. This is done on your computer, on the **Donate documents** page, and saving a donation file puts everything in one `.zip` file.

The file contains:

- **Each document**, as you scanned or photographed it, with any parts you blacked out.
- **The values you checked:** amounts, dates, the store, bank or employer, and the list of rows.
- **What Home Manager read** from the document.
- **A few labels:** the kind of document, photo or scan, and number of pages.

It does not contain your name, your other records, your settings, or anything else from your library. You hand the file to me yourself, on a USB stick or another private channel you choose. Home Manager never sends it anywhere.

## What it's used for

Only to measure how accurately Home Manager's local models read documents, so I can tell whether a change makes it better or worse.

- **Never shown to an AI service.** Your documents are never uploaded to a cloud AI service or read by an AI coding assistant. The tests run on my own computer, with models running on that computer.
- **Never used for training.** They are not used to train or fine-tune any model. If I ever want to do that, I'll ask you separately, and saying no changes nothing here.
- **Never shared.** I don't show them to anyone or publish them. What I may share are counts, such as "2 wrong totals out of 18 receipts". These contain no amounts, names, dates or text from any document.

## How they're kept

- **Where:** on an encrypted drive on my computer, under an ID such as `d07` instead of your name.
- **For how long:** until **[date, e.g. two years from today]**, then I delete them unless you agree to keep them longer.
- **Changing your mind:** tell me at any time and I'll delete your documents and every test result that mentions them. You don't need to give a reason.

## Before you donate

- **Black out what you'd rather not share** before saving the file: account numbers, Social Security numbers, addresses. Do this on the Donate documents page. Blacked-out areas are removed only from the donated copy, never from your own records.
- **Think about other people on the page.** A document can name others, such as a joint account holder, a store clerk or an employer. If you're not comfortable sharing it, leave it out.
- **Mark only what you actually checked.** Anything you didn't look at stays "Not checked" and is never counted.
