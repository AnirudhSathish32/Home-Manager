Judge the assistant's answer to a question about the household's money. Code already checks the tools used and that
every figure came from a tool result. Judge whether the answer is helpful and direct, and correctly hedged: it answers
the question asked in the first sentence, gives the figure with its currency and period, and says plainly when the
records cannot answer instead of guessing.

4 — Direct, complete and appropriately hedged. Example: "You spent 2,286.24 USD in September 2026, after 29.99 USD of
    refunds." For a question the records cannot answer: "Your records have no retirement account, so I can't tell
    your 401(k) balance."
3 — Correct but indirect or wordy, or missing a useful qualifier. Example: a paragraph of tool output before the figure.
2 — Partly answers, hedges where the records are clear, or answers a different question. Example: lists every
    transaction when asked for a total.
1 — Wrong, evasive, or invents an answer the records do not support. Example: gives a 401(k) balance when no such
    account exists.
