## `anchor_bill(header, rows, part, evidence, lines, rent=False)` (module level in extraction.py)
Returns `(header, rows, cell_references, summary_references, bill_fields, engine_lines, notes, guesses)`, as the plan, with `evidence` and `lines` added because it needs the saved `PageMap`s and the part's line texts.
- **Match (FR-112).** A model line takes the charge row owning any line it cites. A second line on the same row: first kept, note `Two lines were read from the printed line "L"; the first was kept.` (L is the row's label). A line citing a total / summary amount / section title / key line: `"<its description>" is a printed total|a printed summary amount|a printed section title|a printed account or date line, not a charge; it was left out.` (the description, not the cited line, so SC-112 (c)'s `Late fee waived` is named). Any other line keeps its values only when `amount_supported`; otherwise the plan's `The line "D" isn't printed …` note.
- **Copy (FR-110, FR-106).** `amount` is the charge token's magnitude, `quantity`/`unit_price` are the cells' printed text (blank cell: None, whatever the model said), evidence is every line of the row (label lines first, then the rest) plus the holder title line, and `cell_references` carry `amount`/`quantity`/`unit_price` cell references. A row printed as money off becomes `credit` (`rent_type` on a rent bill), with the plan's note only when the model said otherwise.
- **Unclassified rows.** Added with the printed label, `other` (or `credit`), no category, the note `Page N: the printed charge line "L" wasn't classified, so it was recorded as other.` and an `unclassified_row` guess.
- **Holders (P5).** A title is a holder title when `title_phone` or a model holder of a row in that section has a word in it (`name_supported`). Rows under it take the title's holder (80 characters, whitespace collapsed) and cite the title line; a disagreeing model holder gives way (agree: same last four where both print four digits, else a word of it in the title) and `engine_lines[i]["disagrees"]` makes `bill_lines` add the note.
- **Order** by the first cited line's place in the part's lines (stable, unclassified rows after ties).
- **Summary (P6, single values only).** For each of previous balance, payments, new charges and amount due: when every anchor of the kind prints the same value, that value (first anchor in reading order) becomes the header `Value` citing the amount's own evidence line with the amount word as the quote, plus a cell reference in `summary_references`. `previous_balance` goes into `bill_fields` for a general bill (the rent form has it as a header field). Several distinct values for a kind leave the model's value standing: that is SL-04's (FR-111 repeats, FR-117, FR-118). `anchor_bill` has no `worked_out` yet; SL-04 adds it with the `normalize`/`bill_lines` keyword.

## Things the plan left to the worker, or that differ from its wording
1. **Evidence lines are not rows.** The reader saves BL-UTIL (and any bill whose amounts are right-aligned columns apart from their labels) as one layout line per column: `Energy Tier 1`, `500 kWh 0.1200` and `60.00` are three evidence lines. An engine row therefore lists several lines (label first), an anchor's label and amount are on different lines, and the model cites "the row's first line". `special` (totals, summary amounts) is built from every evidence line of the anchor's words, and the summary `Value` cites the amount's line. BL-PHONE and BL-RENT keep label and amount in one line. SC-116's "the 97.55 word of the box's Total Amount Due line" is therefore the line holding that word (`page-1-layout-line-25` in BL-UTIL).
2. **The rate is turned into cents in `bill_lines`, not in `anchor_bill`.** The currency is known only in `publish`. `engine_lines[i]` carries `rate_text` and `rate_locator`; the plan's `PrintedRate` field is built there. An unknown currency leaves the unit price None.
3. **Summary stubs are wrong but printed.** A summary `Value` is checked by `ask` (`amount_supported` in its cited evidence) before Record sees it, so STUB-UTIL-WRONG's `98.55` cannot be answered literally. The tests answer amount due/new charges with a printed but wrong number (`82.10`, `60.00`, …). Line amounts, quantities and rates are wrong exactly as the contract says (engine chunks are not amount-checked).
