Assumptions

Spending changes

Raise or lower a category from its recent average, e.g. groceries +10.

Income changes

Added to monthly income from the chosen month on; use a negative amount for a cut.

One-off amounts

Retirement

From the month you retire, take-home pay and contributions from pay stop, and money is drawn from investments: taxable accounts first, then tax-deferred, then tax-free, and an HSA last. Required minimum distributions use the birth year in Settings.

    Paycheck planner

    Other earnings

    Overtime, commission or a regular bonus, per paycheck.

    Bonuses

    Paid once a year with the first paycheck of its month, withheld at the federal supplemental rate (22%).

    Pre-tax deductions

    401(k), HSA, FSA and health, dental and vision premiums. A 401(k) lowers income tax only; the others also lower Social Security and Medicare.

    Federal income tax

    Leave the standard deduction blank to use the year's confirmed table.

    State and local tax

    Leave the state deduction blank to use the state's confirmed table; fill it in where the state gives a personal exemption instead.

    Post-tax deductions

    Paid by your employer

    Not taken from pay; counted toward your 401(k) and HSA.

    Investments

    Retirement, health savings, savings, CDs, Treasuries and brokerage accounts. Values come from your statements, purchase confirmations and what you enter; there are no live prices.

                Model computer

                Vision model

                An image-capable model, such as Qwen3.5-9B. It reads images and scanned pages.

                Reasoning model

                Classifies and extracts saved transcriptions. Independent of the vision model.

                Independent checks

                Laya checks every ledger extraction on this computer. A value it cannot confirm sends the record to review; it never approves one.

                Privacy & security

                • Everything stays on this computer. Home Manager listens only on 127.0.0.1, accepts model servers only at loopback addresses, and sends no telemetry. If you choose a family GPU computer, only page images and text prompts go to it over Tailscale.
                • Evidence is immutable. Every captured file is preserved under its SHA-256 hash; a changed file becomes a new version.
                • Models propose, you decide. Model output stays proposed until you verify it, and totals come from exact arithmetic, never from a model.
                • The session is locked to its launch link. Other programs on this computer can't use the interface without it.

                Back up your library

                Copies every preserved document, reading and the ledger to a folder outside the library, with a checksum manifest. Use a separate drive for real protection.

                StartedResultFiles SizeWhere

                Restore a backup

                Checks a backup against its manifest and rebuilds it as a new library in an empty folder. Your open library is never changed; switch to the restored one in Library folder when you're ready.

                Share your library

                Creates one encrypted .hmshare file of this library: documents, extracted text and ledger. Only someone with the passphrase can open it. Tell them the passphrase another way, such as a phone call, never in the same message as the file.

                At least 12 characters. Four unrelated words are easy to say and hard to guess.

                Open a shared library

                Opens someone's .hmshare file as a temporary copy. Your own library is closed while you look, nothing you do reaches it, and the copy is deleted when you end the session.

                Ask about your household

                Answers come only from your records, through read-only tools, and each figure is checked against what the tools returned. The page you're on is sent as context.

                Ctrl+Enter to send · Ctrl+J to close
